h265_aac_codec12.flv is a legacy HEVC-in-FLV regression fixture.

Source: the existing h265_aac.mp4 (64x64 HEVC, 10 fps, 20 frames,
48 kHz mono AAC, 2 seconds). Audio/video were stream-copied with FFmpeg
7.1.3 to Enhanced FLV. No encoding was performed.

Each hvc1 video tag was converted to the legacy CodecID 12 layout:
- SequenceStart: FrameType | 12, PacketType 0, CTS 0, original hvcC.
- CodedFrames: FrameType | 12, PacketType 1, original CTS and NAL payload.
- CodedFramesX: FrameType | 12, PacketType 1, CTS 0, original NAL payload.
Tag payload lengths and PreviousTagSize fields were recomputed.
Enhanced metadata and script metadata were omitted. The RTMP fixture sends
its own onMetaData with videocodecid 12 and audiocodecid 10.

The stream includes B-frames and nonzero CTS (100, 200, 300, 500 ms), so
the test checks actual HEVC/AAC decode, video frame count and reordered PTS.
FFmpeg 7.1.3 reports "Video codec (c) is not implemented" for this fixture.
