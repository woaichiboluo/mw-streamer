HEVC hardware-decoder fixture, scaled from h265_aac.mp4.
256x144, 10 fps, 20 frames, video only. The original 64x64 HEVC sample is below
the minimum HEVC width supported by the NVIDIA GPU used for these tests.

Generated using FFmpeg 8.1.3:
ffmpeg -i h265_aac.mp4 -map 0:v:0 -vf scale=256:144 -c:v libx265 -preset fast -x265-params log-level=error:pools=1:frame-threads=1 -an h265_cuda.mp4
